Four steps.
No guesswork.

From the moment a shelf is scanned to the moment a shopper finds their product, every piece is designed to be invisible — and reliable.

Scan

Every shelf gets a code

One QR code per shelf. After that, Dalil always knows what's stocked where.

Ask

Tap what you need

No app. No login. A shopper taps the screen and says what they're after.

Navigate

Finds its own way

Dalil reads floor markers, plans the shortest path, and stops safely for people.

Point & dock

Arrives at the shelf

An on-screen arrow shows exactly where to look. Then Dalil returns to base.
