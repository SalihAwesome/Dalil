Four layers.
Pure physics.

AquaCool forces surface water to evaporate, pulling heat directly out of the tank with no refrigerants.

01 · Power

25W Solar + LiFePO4

Monocrystalline solar panel charges a heat-stable battery for round-the-clock cooling.

02 · Filter

Dual-Stage Intake

Washable mesh blocks Shamal sand, then a MERV-13 cartridge keeps air clean.

03 · Pressurize

Forced Evaporation

12V centrifugal blower pushes dry air across the water surface, stripping heat.

04 · Exhaust

Downward Venting

Humid air vents downward through louvers, protecting the solar panel from moisture.
